Privacy Policy — Glowheart Uploader

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Glowheart Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the Glowheart Films YouTube channel (safdarjat45@gmail.com) for managing that owner's own channel.

What the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube to upload videos, set titles, descriptions and tags, add thumbnails and captions, and schedule publication on the owner's own channel.

What data is stored

The app stores only the owner's own OAuth credentials, locally on the owner's own computer, so uploads can be made without signing in each time. No data is sent to any third-party server, no data is sold or shared, and no information about any other person is collected or processed.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions and by deleting the locally stored credentials.
